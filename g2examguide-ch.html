HSI — Hot Surface Igniter / HSI — 热表面点火器
热表面点火器
Energized at 115 VAC for a 17-second warm-up period. Heats to ~900°C to ignite gas at the burners. Replaces standing pilot.
在115 VAC下通电，预热期17秒。加热至约900°C以点燃燃烧器处的燃气。取代了长明火（standing pilot）。
Say this / 这样说
"This is the Hot Surface Igniter. It's energized at 115 volts for approximately 17 seconds to reach ignition temperature — around 900 degrees Celsius. Cold resistance should be between 40 and 70 ohms."
“这是热表面点火器。它在115伏电压下通电约17秒以达到点火温度——大约900摄氏度。冷态电阻应在40到70欧姆之间。”
Gas Valve / Valve Train (GV) / 气阀 / 阀列
气阀 / 阀列
Opens on a 24 VAC signal from the control board via relay GVR. Controls gas flow to the burners. Contains an internal pressure regulator (manifold pressure). Factory set: 3.5" WC for NG.
当控制板通过继电器(GVR)发出24 VAC信号时打开。控制通向燃烧器的燃气流量。包含一个内部压力调节器（歧管压力）。出厂设置：天然气(NG)为3.5" WC。
Say this / 这样说
"This is the gas valve. It opens when the control board energizes the gas valve relay — GVR — supplying 24 volts to the valve coil. It also contains the internal manifold pressure regulator."
“这是气阀。当控制板激发气阀继电器（GVR），向阀线圈提供24伏电压时，它就会打开。它还包含内部的歧管压力调节器。”
HPS — Housing Pressure Switch / HPS — 壳体压力开关
壳体压力开关（主压力开关）
Normally Open (N.O.) contact. Closes when inducer motor creates sufficient negative pressure (draft). Ignored for the first minute after gas valve closes per the Carrier sequence. Nominal break point: 0.63–0.73" WC.
常开（N.O.）触点。当引风机电机产生足够的负压（抽力）时闭合。根据Carrier的顺序，在气阀关闭后的第一分钟内会被忽略。标称断开点：0.63–0.73" WC。
Say this / 这样说
"This is the housing pressure switch — HPS. It's a normally-open switch that closes when the inducer creates sufficient negative pressure in the collector box, signalling the control board that it's safe to proceed with ignition."
“这是壳体压力开关（HPS）。它是一个常开开关，当引风机在集流箱中产生足够的负压时闭合，向控制板发出信号表明可以安全进行点火。”
LPS — Low Gas Pressure Switch / LPS — 低气压保护开关
低气压保护开关（丙烷系统）
Used on propane systems. Opens if inlet gas pressure drops below safe minimum, preventing incomplete combustion. Factory connected only when LGPS is used.
用于丙烷系统。如果入口燃气压力降至安全最低值以下，它会断开，从而防止不完全燃烧。出厂时仅在使用LGPS时连接。
Say this / 这样说
"This is the Low Gas Pressure Switch — LGPS — primarily used on propane systems. It opens if the supply gas pressure falls below the minimum required, shutting down the furnace to prevent incomplete combustion."
“这是低燃气压力开关（LGPS），主要用于丙烷系统。如果供气压力低于所需的最低值，它就会断开，关闭炉子以防止不完全燃烧。”
Pressure Switch (Collector Box — LPS) / 压力开关（集流箱）
集流箱压力开关
First pressure switch to close as inducer comes up to speed. Begins the 15-second pre-purge. Labeled "COLLECTOR BOX-LPS" on the switch. Nominal break: 0.63" WC min.
引风机提速时第一个闭合的压力开关。开始15秒的预吹扫。开关上标有"COLLECTOR BOX-LPS"。标称断开点：最低0.63" WC。
Say this / 这样说
"This pressure switch — labeled COLLECTOR BOX LPS — is the first to close as the inducer motor comes up to speed, initiating the 15-second pre-purge period."
“这个标有 COLLECTOR BOX LPS 的压力开关，是引风机电机提速时第一个闭合的开关，用于启动15秒的预吹扫期。”
Flame Sensor / FSE / 火焰传感器
火焰传感器
Uses flame rectification to prove burner ignition. Control board reads 4.0–8.0 µA DC (nominal). If flame not proved within 2 seconds, gas valve closes. Retries up to 3 times → Lockout Code 14. Clean with 0000 steel wool only.
利用火焰整流原理来证明燃烧器点火。控制板读取4.0–8.0 µA DC（标称值）。如果2秒内未探测到火焰，气阀关闭。最多重试3次 → 锁定代码14。仅使用0000号钢丝棉清洁。
Say this / 这样说
"This is the flame sensing electrode — FSE. It uses flame rectification to prove ignition. The control board looks for 4 to 8 microamps DC. If flame is not proved within 2 seconds, the gas valve closes and the sequence retries up to 3 times before ignition lockout."
“这是火焰传感电极（FSE）。它利用火焰整流来证明点火。控制板寻找4到8微安的直流电。如果2秒内未探测到火焰，气阀将关闭，并在点火锁定前最多重试3次。”
Control Board / PCB / 控制板 / 电路板
控制板 / 电路板
The "brain" of the furnace. Controls entire sequence of operation, monitors all safety switches, displays 2-digit LED fault codes. Contains 3-amp automotive-type blade fuse (SEC-1 & SEC-2 connections = 24V secondary). Status LED is amber.
炉子的“大脑”。控制整个操作顺序，监控所有安全开关，显示2位LED故障代码。包含3安培汽车型插片式保险丝（SEC-1和SEC-2连接 = 24V次级）。状态LED为琥珀色。
Say this / 这样说
"This is the furnace control board. It manages the entire sequence of operation, monitors all safety switch inputs, and displays 2-digit LED fault codes visible through the blower door window. It contains a 3-amp blade fuse protecting the 24-volt secondary circuit."
“这是炉子的控制板。它管理整个操作顺序，监控所有安全开关的输入，并通过鼓风机门窗显示2位LED故障代码。它包含一个保护24伏次级电路的3安培插片式保险丝。”
Limit Switches — LS1, LS2 / 过热限位开关
过热限位开关
Auto-reset. Opens on over-temperature condition. Blower runs for up to 4 minutes to cool exchanger when limit trips (Code 33). Manual-reset flame rollout switch is also on burner assembly — requires physical button reset.
自动复位。在超温情况下断开。当限位开关跳闸（代码33）时，鼓风机会运行长达4分钟来冷却热交换器。燃烧器组件上还有手动复位的火焰外溢（rollout）开关——需要物理按下按钮复位。
Say this / 这样说
"These are the limit switches — LS1 and LS2. They are auto-reset and open on over-temperature. When a limit trips, the blower continues to run for up to 4 minutes to cool the heat exchanger. The flame rollout switch on the burner assembly, by contrast, requires manual reset."
“这些是限位开关（LS1和LS2）。它们是自动复位的，在超温时断开。当限位开关跳闸时，鼓风机会继续运行长达4分钟以冷却热交换器。相比之下，燃烧器组件上的火焰外溢开关需要手动复位。”
Inducer Motor (IDM) / 引风机
引风机 / 诱导送风机
First component energized in every heating call. Creates negative pressure to close pressure switches. Also provides post-purge for 15 seconds after gas valve closes. 115 VAC motor.
每次供热呼叫时第一个通电的组件。产生负压以闭合压力开关。还在气阀关闭后提供15秒的后吹扫。115 VAC 电机。
Say this / 这样说
"This is the inducer draft motor — IDM. It is the first component energized on every call for heat. It creates negative pressure to close the pressure switches and also runs for a 15-second post-purge after the gas valve closes."
“这是诱导引风机电机（IDM）。它是每次供热呼叫时第一个通电的组件。它产生负压以闭合压力开关，并在气阀关闭后运行15秒进行后吹扫。”
Transformer (TRAN) / 变压器
变压器
Steps 115 VAC (primary, PR-1) down to 24 VAC (secondary, SEC-1 & SEC-2) for the control circuit. SEC-1 feeds the 3-amp fuse.
将115 VAC（初级，PR-1）降压至24 VAC（次级，SEC-1 & SEC-2）供控制电路使用。SEC-1为3安培保险丝供电。
Say this / 这样说
"This is the transformer. It steps 115 volts line voltage down to 24 volts for the low-voltage control circuit. The primary is 115 VAC; the secondary feeds the 3-amp fuse and the control board."
“这是变压器。它将115伏的主电源电压降至24伏，用于低压控制电路。初级是115 VAC；次级为3安培保险丝和控制板供电。”
Condensate Trap / 冷凝水收集器
冷凝水收集器
Unique to condensing furnaces. Collects acidic condensate from secondary heat exchanger. MUST be primed with 1 quart (1 litre) of water before first startup. Drain line: min 1/4" per foot slope.
冷凝炉所独有。收集来自二次热交换器的酸性冷凝水。在首次启动前必须注入1夸脱（1升）水作为引水。排水管：每英尺至少1/4英寸的坡度。
Say this / 这样说
"This is the condensate trap — specific to condensing furnaces. It collects acidic condensate from the secondary heat exchanger and must be primed with one quart of water before startup. The condensate drain line requires a minimum slope of one-quarter inch per foot toward the drain."
“这是冷凝水收集器（存水弯）——冷凝炉专用。它收集来自二次热交换器的酸性冷凝水，在启动前必须注入一夸脱的引水。冷凝水排水管需要朝向排水口有每英尺至少四分之一英寸的坡度。”
Blower Motor (BLWM) / 循环风机
循环风机
Starts 25 seconds after flame is proved (blower-ON delay). Distributes conditioned air through duct system. After thermostat is satisfied: runs for blower-OFF delay — factory set 120 seconds.
在火焰被确认后25秒启动（风机开启延迟）。通过管道系统分配调节好的空气。恒温器满足后：运行以完成风机关闭延迟——出厂设置为120秒。
Say this / 这样说
"This is the circulating blower motor. It starts 25 seconds after flame is proved to allow the heat exchanger to warm up. After the thermostat is satisfied, it continues for the blower-off delay — factory set at 120 seconds — to extract residual heat from the exchanger."
“这是循环鼓风机电机。在探测到火焰25秒后它会启动，让热交换器有时间预热。当恒温器满足（达到设定温度）后，它会继续运行以完成风机关闭延迟（出厂设定为120秒），以提取热交换器中残留的热量。”

Heating Mode — from manual p.69 / 供热模式 — 摘自手册 p.69
1
Thermostat calls for heat — R-to-W circuit closes
恒温器发出供热呼叫 — R到W电路闭合
Say this / 这样说
"The wall thermostat closes the R-to-W circuit, applying 24 volts to the furnace control board. The board performs a self-check, confirming the collector box pressure switch LPS and housing pressure switch HPS are both open."
“墙壁恒温器闭合R到W电路，向炉子控制板施加24伏电压。控制板执行自检，确认集流箱压力开关LPS和壳体压力开关HPS都处于断开状态。”
2
Inducer Motor starts — Pre-Purge Period (15 sec)
引风机电机启动 — 预吹扫阶段 (15秒)
Say this / 这样说
"The control board energizes the inducer motor IDM. As it comes up to speed, the collector box pressure switch LPS closes, beginning a 15-second pre-purge period to clear the heat exchanger of any residual gases."
“控制板给引风机电机IDM通电。随着电机提速，集流箱压力开关LPS闭合，开始15秒的预吹扫，以清除热交换器中残留的任何气体。”
3
HPS closes — HSI Warm-Up (17 sec)
HPS闭合 — HSI预热 (17秒)
Say this / 这样说
"At the end of the pre-purge period, the inducer housing pressure switch HPS closes, confirming adequate draft. The hot surface igniter HSI is then energized at 115 volts for a 17-second warm-up period."
“在预吹扫期结束时，引风机壳体压力开关HPS闭合，确认有足够的抽力。然后热表面点火器HSI在115伏电压下通电，进行17秒的预热。”
4
Trial-for-Ignition — Gas Valve opens
尝试点火 — 气阀打开
Say this / 这样说
"When the igniter warm-up is complete, the main gas valve relay GVR closes, energizing the gas valve GV. Gas flows to the burners and is ignited by the HSI. 24 VAC is also supplied to the humidifier terminal HUM at this point."
“当点火器预热完成，主气阀继电器GVR闭合，给气阀GV通电。燃气流向燃烧器，并被HSI点燃。此时，也会向加湿器端子HUM提供24 VAC电压。”
5
Flame Proving — FSE confirms ignition (2 sec window)
火焰探测 — FSE确认点火（2秒窗口）
Say this / 这样说
"Five seconds after GVR closes, the 2-second flame proving period begins. The flame sensor FSE uses flame rectification to confirm the burner flame is present. If flame is not proved, the gas valve closes and the board retries the ignition sequence up to 3 more times before going to Ignition Lockout — Code 14."
“GVR闭合5秒后，开始2秒的火焰探测期。火焰传感器FSE利用火焰整流来确认燃烧器火焰是否存在。如果未探测到火焰，气阀关闭，控制板在进入点火锁定（代码14）之前最多再重试3次点火程序。”
6
Blower-ON Delay (25 sec) — BLWM starts on HEAT speed
风机开启延迟 (25秒) — BLWM以供热转速启动
Say this / 这样说
"When flame is proved, the control board starts the blower-ON delay — 25 seconds. This allows the heat exchanger to warm up before distributing air. After 25 seconds, the blower motor starts on the HEAT speed tap."
“确认火焰后，控制板启动25秒的风机开启延迟。这让热交换器在分配空气之前有时间预热。25秒后，鼓风机电机在供热(HEAT)转速挡启动。”
7
Thermostat satisfied — Blower-OFF Delay (120 sec default)
恒温器满足 — 风机关闭延迟（默认120秒）
Say this / 这样说
"When the thermostat is satisfied, the R-to-W circuit opens, de-energizing gas valve GV and the humidifier terminal. The inducer runs for a 15-second post-purge. The blower motor and EAC-1 terminal remain energized for the blower-OFF delay — factory set at 120 seconds — then shut down."
“当恒温器达到设定温度时，R到W电路断开，使气阀GV和加湿器端子断电。引风机运行15秒进行后吹扫。鼓风机电机和EAC-1端子继续通电运行以完成风机关闭延迟（出厂设定为120秒），然后关机。”

What is ULC S636? / 什么是 ULC S636？
Say this / 这样说
"ULC S636 is the Canadian certification standard for Category IV — positive-pressure condensing — venting systems. Per CAN/CSA B149.1, all pipe, fittings, primer, and cement must be ULC S636 listed AND from the same manufacturer. You cannot mix brands of cement and pipe."
“ULC S636 是加拿大 IV 类（正压冷凝）排气系统的认证标准。根据 CAN/CSA B149.1，所有管道、管件、底漆和胶水必须获得 ULC S636 认证，且必须来自同一制造商。你不能混用不同品牌的胶水和管道。”

1
Measure and cut pipe to required length using appropriate saw.

2
Deburr the inside edge and chamfer (bevel) the outside edge of the cut end for better cement distribution.
去除切口内侧的毛刺，并在外侧边缘倒角，以便胶水更好地分布。
3
Dry-fit all pieces. Mark insertion depth on pipe with a pencil.
预先干插（无胶）组装所有部件。用铅笔在管子上标记插入深度。

允许排气长度 & 等效长度
▶
Say this — Allowable Vent Length / 这样说 — 关于允许排气长度
"Allowable vent length is the maximum length of venting pipe permitted by the manufacturer for a specific appliance model and pipe diameter. For this furnace, it is found in Table 13 — Maximum Equivalent Vent Length. Exceeding this length reduces draft, causes poor combustion, nuisance lockouts, and potential vent freeze-up."
“允许排气长度是制造商针对特定设备型号和管径所允许的排气管最大长度。对于这款炉子，可以在表13（最大等效排气长度）中找到。超过这个长度会降低抽力，导致燃烧不良、频繁锁定，并可能造成排气口结冰。”

"Equivalent length accounts for the additional flow resistance created by elbows and fittings. A 90-degree long-radius elbow, for example, adds 3 feet of equivalent resistance. “等效长度考虑了弯头和管件造成的额外气流阻力。例如，一个90度长半径弯头会增加3英尺的等效阻力。一个斜接90度弯头会增加8英尺。我们将等效长度与实际管道长度相加，得到总等效排气长度(TEVL)，然后将其与最大等效排气长度(MEVL)进行比较。”

自然通风炉工作原理
▶
Say this — Overview / 这样说 — 概述
"A natural draft furnace uses an atmospheric burner that draws combustion air by natural convection. Primary air is mixed with gas at the burner orifice through a venturi effect. Secondary air surrounds the flame and completes combustion. Hot combustion products rise by natural buoyancy up through the heat exchanger, through the draft diverter, and up the flue — no fan is required for venting. Efficiency is approximately 60 to 80 percent AFUE..."
“自然通风炉使用大气式燃烧器，通过自然对流吸入燃烧空气。一次空气通过文丘里效应在燃烧器喷嘴处与燃气混合。二次空气包围火焰并完成燃烧。热燃烧产物通过自然浮力上升，穿过热交换器、调风板，并顺着烟道排出——排气不需要引风机。其效率约为 60% 到 80% AFUE...”
Draft Diverter — Purpose & Smoke Test / 调风板 — 作用与烟雾测试
Say this — Draft Diverter / 这样说 — 关于调风板
"The draft diverter serves three functions: it relieves sudden down-drafts that could extinguish the pilot flame; it dilutes flue gases with room air to prevent chimney deterioration; and it maintains a constant, stable draft at the heat exchanger regardless of variations in chimney pull."
“调风板有三个功能：它可以缓解可能吹灭长明火的突然下行气流（倒灌）；它用室内空气稀释烟气以防止烟囱老化损坏；并且无论烟囱的抽力如何变化，它都能在热交换器处保持恒定、稳定的抽力。”
Say this — Smoke / Spillage Test / 这样说 — 烟雾/溢出测试
"To perform the spillage test, I run the furnace for at least 5 minutes, then hold a smoke match or candle at the relief opening of the draft diverter. If smoke is drawn in — toward the furnace — draft is functioning correctly. If smoke is pushed out — spillage — combustion gases are entering the living space. I must immediately shut down the furnace and investigate the cause."
“执行溢出测试时，我让炉子运行至少5分钟，然后在调风板的释放口处拿一根烟雾火柴或蜡烛。如果烟雾被吸入（朝向炉子），说明抽力运作正常。如果烟雾被推出来（溢出），说明燃烧气体正在进入生活空间。我必须立即关闭炉子并调查原因。”

Say this — Function / 这样说 — 关于功能
"The heat anticipator is a small adjustable resistor inside a mechanical thermostat... It generates a small amount of heat directly on the bimetal, causing the thermostat to shut off the furnace slightly before the room reaches the set point. This compensates for the residual heat stored in the heat exchanger, preventing room temperature overshoot."
“热预感器是机械恒温器内部的一个小可调电阻... 它直接在双金属片上产生少量热量，使恒温器在室温达到设定点之前稍微提前一点关闭炉子。这补偿了储存在热交换器中的残余热量，防止了室温超调（过冲）。”
How to Set the Anticipator — Ammeter Method / 如何设置预感器 — 安培计测量法
1
Remove thermostat from sub-base (do not disconnect wires).
从底座上取下恒温器本体（不要断开接线）。
2
Connect a hook-around ammeter across the R and W sub-base terminals by wrapping the wire 10 turns around the ammeter jaws.
通过将导线在钳形安培计钳口上缠绕10圈，跨接底座上的 R 和 W 端子。
3
Record ammeter reading. Divide by 10 (because of 10 turns) to get true current.
记录安培计读数。除以10（因为绕了10圈）得到真实电流值。
4
Set anticipator dial to that value.
将热预感器拨盘设置到该真实数值上。

“我正将 R 到 W 的导线在我的钳形表钳口上绕10圈，以放大微弱的电流读数。仪表上读数是 5 安培——除以10圈，实际电流是 0.5 安培。我将把热预感器设置为 0.5。”

Say this — Grounding and Bonding / 这样说 — 关于接地与等电位联结
"Grounding connects the electrical system to earth ground at the main panel... Bonding connects all metallic components — gas piping, electrical panel, ductwork, water piping — together so they are at the same electrical potential. This prevents dangerous voltage differences that could cause shock or sparks near gas piping."
“接地（Grounding）是在主配电盘处将电气系统连接到地球大地... 等电位联结（Bonding）是将所有金属部件——燃气管道、配电盘、金属风管、水管——连接在一起，使它们处于相同的电势/电位。这防止了危险的电压差，避免在燃气管道附近产生电击或火花。”
