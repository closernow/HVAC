零件识别 / Component ID
指出实物 + 说出功能
▶
⚡ 考官要求你指向实物并说明功能。手指明确指向部件，边指边说英文。
HSI (Hot Surface Igniter)
🔥 热表面点火器
通电后加热至约900°C，点燃燃烧器气体。取代传统长明火pilot。
"This is the Hot Surface Igniter. It heats to approximately 900°C to ignite the gas at the burners. Resistance should be 40–70 ohms when cold."
Gas Valve (Valve Train)
⚙️ 气阀 / 阀列
接收控制板24V信号后开启，允许天然气或丙烷流向燃烧器。包含压力调节功能。
"This is the gas valve. It opens on a 24V signal from the control board and allows gas to flow to the burners. It also has an internal pressure regulator."
HPS — Housing Pressure Switch
💨 壳体压力开关（主压力开关）
验证引风机已运转并产生足够负压。正常运行时闭合（N.O.接点）。若堵塞或引风机故障则保持断开，锁定点火。
"This is the housing pressure switch, or HPS. It verifies the inducer motor is creating sufficient negative pressure before allowing ignition to proceed."
LPS — Low Gas Pressure Switch
🔽 低气压保护开关（丙烷专用）
丙烷系统专用，气压过低时断开，防止不完全燃烧。天然气系统通常无此开关。
"This is the Low Gas Pressure Switch, used primarily on propane systems. It opens if inlet gas pressure drops too low, preventing incomplete combustion."
Flame Sensor / FSE
🔦 火焰传感器
通过火焰整流原理，检测燃烧器是否点燃。若点火后2秒内无火焰信号，控制板关闭气阀，重试3次后锁定。
"This is the flame sensing electrode. It uses flame rectification to confirm burner ignition. If no signal within 2 seconds, the gas valve closes and re-tries up to 3 times."
Control Board (PCB)
🖥️ 控制板 / 电路板
炉子的"大脑"，控制整个启动顺序、故障诊断和LED故障码显示。含3A保险丝。
"This is the furnace control board — the brain of the system. It controls the entire sequence of operation, displays diagnostic LED codes, and contains the 3-amp fuse."
Limit Switch (LS1, LS2)
🌡️ 过热限位开关
热交换器温度超过设定值（强制送风炉约200°F）时自动断开，切断气阀，保护炉体不过热。手动复位款需按键复位。
"This is the high limit switch. It opens on over-temperature — set at approximately 200°F for a forced-warm-air furnace — cutting the gas valve to prevent overheating."
Inducer Motor (IDM)
💨 引风机（诱导送风机）
首先启动，强制排出燃烧废气，同时产生负压使压力开关闭合。高效冷凝炉独有，取代传统烟囱浮力通风。
"This is the inducer draft motor. It starts first in the sequence, forces combustion gases out the vent, and creates the negative pressure that closes the pressure switch."
Transformer (TRAN)
⚡ 变压器
将115V市电降压为24V，供控制回路使用。Primary（初级）115V，Secondary（次级）24V。
"This is the transformer. It steps down 115V line voltage to 24V for the control circuit. Primary side is 115V, secondary side is 24V."
Condensate Trap
💧 冷凝水收集器
高效冷凝炉专有。二次热交换产生的酸性冷凝水（pH约3-4）收集在此，通过CPVC管排出。必须用水预灌（prime）。
"This is the condensate trap — unique to condensing furnaces. It collects acidic condensate from the secondary heat exchanger and must be primed with water before startup."
Blower Motor (BLWM)
🌀 循环风机
热交换器加热后（约25秒），送热风进入管道。满足温控后继续运行（blower-off delay：120秒默认）带走余热。
"This is the circulating blower motor. It starts approximately 25 seconds after ignition to distribute heated air. After the thermostat is satisfied, it continues for the blower-off delay period — 120 seconds by default."
Flame Rollout Switch
🔥 火焰外溢开关（手动复位）
位于燃烧器组件上，当火焰异常外溢时触发，切断气阀。必须手动按键复位。触发原因：通风受阻、气压过高等。
"This is the manual-reset flame rollout switch, located on the burner assembly. It trips if flame rolls out of the heat exchanger — indicating blocked venting or excessive gas pressure."

操作顺序 / Sequence of Operation
必须按顺序说出来 — Carrier PG95ESA
▶
💡 口诀：T-I-P-H-G-F-B = Thermostat → Inducer → Pressure switch → HSI → Gas valve → Flame prove → Blower
1
恒温器呼叫供热 (Thermostat calls for heat)
"The wall thermostat closes the R-to-W circuit, sending a 24V call for heat to the furnace control board. The board performs a self-check, verifying the pressure switch contacts LPS and HPS are open."
W端到控制板，24V信号。R端是24V电源热端，W是供热请求。
2
引风机启动 — Inducer Pre-Purge (15秒)
"The control board energizes the inducer draft motor. As it comes up to speed, the collector box pressure switch LPS closes, beginning a 15-second pre-purge period."
引风机先启动，清除炉腔内残余气体，产生负压使压力开关闭合。
3
HSI预热 — Igniter Warm-Up (17秒)
"After the 15-second pre-purge, the housing pressure switch HPS closes, confirming adequate draft. The Hot Surface Igniter is then energized for a 17-second warm-up period."
HPS闭合后HSI才通电。HSI需要17秒加热至点火温度。
4
气阀开启 — Gas Valve Opens
"When the igniter warm-up is complete, the control board closes the gas valve relay GVR, energizing the gas valve GV. Gas flows to the burners and is ignited by the HSI."
控制板给气阀继电器通电，24V加到气阀线圈，气阀开启。
5
火焰确认 — Flame Proving (2秒)
"The flame sensor detects the burner flame by flame rectification. The control board begins the blower-on delay period of 25 seconds. If flame is not proven within 2 seconds, the gas valve closes and the sequence retries — up to 3 times before Ignition Lockout Code 14."
2秒内没有火焰信号 = 气阀关闭，重试，3次后锁定（故障码14）。
6
循环风机启动 — Blower-On Delay (25秒)
"25 seconds after gas valve opens and flame is proven, the blower motor starts on HEAT speed to distribute warm air throughout the duct system."
25秒延迟让热交换器先升温，避免冷风吹出。
7
满足温控 / Blower-Off Delay
"When the thermostat is satisfied, the R-to-W circuit opens. The gas valve de-energizes and closes. The inducer runs for a 15-second post-purge. The blower motor continues for the blower-off delay — 120 seconds by default — then shuts down."
引风机后冲15秒清余气，风机继续120秒（默认）带走余热。

故障排查 / Troubleshooting
考官会预设故障，参照电路图(Fig.71)逐步测电压
▶
📢 排查开场白
"I'll reference the schematic diagram to understand the normal sequence of operation, then trace the circuit from the point of failure using voltage measurements, working from the power source toward the load."
🔴 Open High Limit (高限位断路) LED 33
现象：炉子不启动或运行中途停止，风机持续转4分钟
测量：测limit switch两端电压 → 正常：0V（闭合）；故障：24V（断路）
原因：过热（气流受阻/过滤网脏/风机慢），或limit开关本身坏
"I'm measuring across the high limit switch. I'm reading 24V, which tells me the switch is open — the limit has tripped due to an over-temperature condition or a failed switch."
🔴 HSI Failure (点火器故障) LED 14/34
现象：引风机运转，但炉子不点火，3次重试后锁定
测量：通电测HSI：应有115V；断电测电阻：正常40-70Ω，断路=∞
"With power on, I'm measuring voltage at the HSI connector — I see 115V, so the board is commanding the igniter. Now I'll de-energize and check resistance — it reads open circuit, so the igniter itself has failed."
🔴 Pressure Switch Failure LED 31
现象：引风机转，但控制板序列不继续，HSI不通电
测量：引风机运转时测PS两端：正常应为0V（闭合）；故障：24V（断路）
检查：压力管堵塞/扭曲，冷凝水堵在管内，PS开关本身坏
🔴 Open Neutral Wire (中性线断路)
现象：炉子完全无响应，LED不亮
测量：测transformer次级（SEC-1/SEC-2）：应有24V；测L1/L2：应有115V
若L2无电：查断路器，查中性线连接
🔴 Broken "W" Wire (W线断路)
现象：恒温器呼叫但炉子无响应，24V系统正常
测量：在控制板W端测24V对COM：应有24V（呼叫时）；若0V = W线断或恒温器问题
🔴 Inducer Motor Failure (引风机故障) LED 31
现象：无引风机声音，PS不闭合
测量：测IDM电机端：有115V但不转 = 电机坏；无115V = 控制板问题

什么是 ULC S636？

ULC S636是加拿大对Category IV（正压/冷凝型）排气系统的认证标准。在加拿大，所有高效冷凝炉和热水器的PVC/ABS通风管道必须符合此标准。管道、胶水、底漆必须来自同一厂商。

📢 解释S636
"ULC S636 is the Canadian certification standard for Category IV venting systems — that is, positive-pressure, condensing appliances. The pipe, fittings, primer, and cement must all be from the same manufacturer and ULC S636 listed. In Canada, CAN/CSA B149 requires this for condensing appliances."

等效长度计算 / Equivalent Vent Length
▶

TEVL = Total Equivalent Vent Length

1
量出每根管道的实际长度（从炉子到终端）
2
统计弯头数量和类型（90°还是45°，long/medium/mitered）
3
每个弯头乘以其等效长度（查Table 14）：90° Long Radius = 3ft，Medium = 5ft，Mitered = 8ft
4
TEVL = 管道总长 + 所有弯头等效长度
5
与MEVL（Table 13查最大等效长度）对比：TEVL < MEVL = ✅ 允许
📢 解释等效长度
"The equivalent length accounts for the resistance created by elbows and fittings. A 90-degree long-radius elbow, for example, adds 3 feet of equivalent resistance to the vent system. We add these to the actual pipe length to get the Total Equivalent Vent Length, and verify it does not exceed the Maximum Equivalent Vent Length for this appliance and pipe size."
📢 解释超标后果
"If we exceed the manufacturer's maximum allowable vent length, or fail to account for equivalent lengths of fittings, the system will not have adequate draft — leading to incomplete combustion, nuisance lockouts, condensate freeze-up at the termination, and potential carbon monoxide spillage into the structure."

安装操作步骤 / Installation Procedure
▶
1
参照说明书确认此炉型允许的最大等效长度（MEVL，Table 13）
2
量管、切管、去毛边（deburr）、倒角（chamfer）
3
干插试合（dry fit），在管端标记插入深度
4

5
涂胶水（cement）— 管端和插口内部均匀涂抹
6
插入后旋转¼圈（quarter turn），保持30秒
7
水平排气管向炉子方向坡下 ≥ ¼"每英尺（min slope back）
8
按Table 16用管夹支撑（PVC 3ft间距）
9
用水平仪确认坡度
⚠️ 加拿大：底漆和胶水必须与管道同一厂商，均须ULC S636认证。不能使用不同厂商的材料。
📢 演示排气坡度
"I'm using a level to verify the slope of this horizontal vent section. Per the manufacturer's instructions, horizontal vent pipe must slope downward toward the furnace at a minimum of one-quarter inch per linear foot. This ensures condensate drains back to the furnace rather than pooling in the pipe, which could block the vent or cause freeze-up."

🔥
热电偶系统原理 / Thermocouple Basics
▶
📢 解释热电偶原理
"A thermocouple is a safety device that generates a small millivolt EMF — typically 25 to 30 millivolts — when its tip is heated by the pilot flame. This voltage holds open the gas valve safety electromagnet. If the pilot goes out, the thermocouple cools, the voltage drops, and the safety magnet releases, closing the gas valve to prevent unburned gas from escaping."
25–30mVOpen Circuit 开路电压
≥10mVClosed Circuit 闭路电压（最小）
2–4mVDrop-Out 断开压降
90秒Gas Code 断开时间要求
🔬
三项测试操作 / Three Tests
▶

1. 开路测试 (Open Circuit Test)

准备
长明火正常燃烧，万用表设到DC mV档
测量
直接在热电偶接头处测量（不接气阀）
读数
正常：25–30mV；低于25mV = 热电偶弱或pilot火焰位置不对
"I'm measuring the open-circuit output of the thermocouple at the connection fitting. I'm reading 28mV, which is within the normal range of 25 to 30 millivolts. This indicates the thermocouple is generating adequate voltage."

2. 闭路测试 (Closed Circuit / Load Test)

准备
安装热电偶适配器，将热电偶接回气阀但通过适配器引出测量点
测量
在适配器处测量（有负载/under load）
读数
应为开路电压的约一半：≥10mV；若低于10mV = 热电偶弱或气阀电磁铁有问题
"For the closed-circuit test, I'm using the thermocouple adaptor to measure voltage under load — with the thermocouple connected to the gas valve. The reading should be at least half the open-circuit value, and not less than 10 millivolts."

3. 断开测试 (Drop-Out Test)

步骤
pilot正常燃烧时，手动熄灭pilot（如盖住或关闭pilot旋钮）
计时
从pilot熄灭到气阀关闭（听到"咔哒"声）
要求
Gas Code要求 ≤ 90秒内关闭；此时电压降至2–4mV
"For the drop-out test, I'll extinguish the pilot and time how long it takes for the gas valve to close. The Gas Code requires this to occur within 90 seconds. During drop-out, the voltage will fall to approximately 2 to 4 millivolts."

Pilot火焰特征 / Pilot Flame Characteristics
▶
📢 描述理想pilot火焰
"A proper pilot flame should be steady and strong — blue in color if aerated, or luminous/yellow-orange if not aerated. The flame should envelope the thermocouple tip by approximately one-half to three-eighths of an inch. A weak, flickering, or lifting flame will produce inadequate thermocouple voltage."

📊 /40分  |  S6 — Gas Pressure Regulators
⚙️
调节器五大组件 / 5 Regulator Components
▶
Loading Element (Spring)
🔩 负载弹簧
提供设定压力的参考力。顺时针旋紧弹簧 = 增加出口压力；逆时针 = 降低压力。
"The loading element is the spring. Turning the adjustment screw clockwise compresses the spring and increases outlet pressure."
Measuring Element (Diaphragm)
🔵 测量膜片
感应出口压力变化，与弹簧力对比平衡，控制阀口开度。
"The diaphragm is the measuring element. It senses outlet pressure and moves in response to changes — balancing against the spring force."
Restricting Element (Orifice)
🔸 节流元件（孔板/阀座）
实际限制气体流量的部件。膜片位移控制其开度大小，从而调节出口压力。
"The restricting element — a disk, valve seat, or plug — actually controls the gas flow. The diaphragm movement changes how much this orifice opens or closes."
Atmospheric Vent
🌬️ 大气排气口
允许膜片下方的空间与大气相通，确保膜片能自由移动。若堵塞 = 调节器不能正常工作。
"The atmospheric vent allows the space below the diaphragm to communicate with atmosphere, enabling the diaphragm to move freely in response to pressure changes."
Block Vent Effect
🚫 堵塞通气口效果
若大气排气口被堵（如积冰、昆虫筑巢），调节器无法正确调压，可能导致出口压力过高或过低。
"If the atmospheric vent is blocked — by ice, insects, or debris — the regulator cannot properly sense outlet pressure. This can cause outlet pressure to increase dramatically, potentially overfiring the appliance."

清管（Purging）规定
▶
📢 解释清管工具使用限制（Gas Code）
"Per CAN/CSA B149.1, a purging tool — or purging device — may only be used where the purge volume is less than the maximum allowed by code. The tool must not create an ignition source. 漏气测试 / Leak Testing
▶
📢 皂液测试（Soap Test）
"I'm applying a commercially available leak detection solution to all gas connections and fittings. I'll observe each joint for bubble formation, which would indicate a gas leak. I'll never use an open flame to test for leaks."

气表拨盘测试（Dial Test）

1
所有气用设备关闭（所有用气点OFF）
2
找最小表盘（smallest dial）
3
用胶带标记当前指针位置（tape mark）
4
等待10分钟
5
若指针移动 = 有泄漏！
📢 解释拨盘测试
"For the dial test, I've closed all gas appliances. I'm using the smallest test dial on the meter — the 2 or 5 cubic foot dial — and marking the pointer position with tape. After 10 minutes, if the needle has moved, this indicates gas is flowing, meaning there is a leak somewhere in the system."
💡 拨盘测试何时可以进行：Gas Code规定，只有在供气压力 ≤ ½ psig (14" WC) 时才能进行拨盘测试（不能在高压测试中进行）。
🕐
气表计时 / Clocking the Gas Meter
▶
📢 解释计时公式
"To clock the appliance input, I'll turn off all other gas appliances, run this furnace in heating mode, and time how long it takes for the test dial to complete one full revolution. I'll do this 3 times and take the average."

计算公式

Gas Rate (ft³/hr) = 3600 ÷ 秒数 × 表盘容积(ft³)
BTU/h = ft³/hr × 1,000 (天然气近似值) 或 × 35,310 (m³/hr转BTU/h)
"For example: if using the 2-cubic-foot test dial, and it takes 30 seconds for one revolution: Gas Rate = 3600 ÷ 30 × 2 = 240 cubic feet per hour. Multiplied by the heating value of natural gas — approximately 1,000 BTU per cubic foot — gives 240,000 BTU/h input. I'll compare this to the nameplate input rating."
💡 过开（Over-firing）：实际输入 > 铭牌额定值 = 热交换器过热，缩短寿命
欠开（Under-firing）：实际输入 < 铭牌额定值 = 加热不足，冷凝水问题
允许范围：额定值 ±2%

📢 解释dirt pocket
"A sediment trap — or dirt pocket — is required by the Gas Code upstream of each appliance gas control valve. It's constructed by installing a tee in the supply riser, with a capped nipple extending below the tee. Any scale, moisture, or debris falls into the trap rather than entering the gas valve, which could cause the valve to stick open or closed."
📢 解释管道密封剂涂抹
"I'm applying pipe dope sparingly to the male threads only — the external threads. Applying it to the female threads or socket can contaminate the gas valve. The sealant must be approved for use with the specific gas being used — propane requires a propane-resistant compound."

"The grounding conductor connects the electrical system to earth ground at the main panel, protecting against fault currents and lightning. 120V vs 240V 识别

120V
使用单极断路器（single pole），接一根hot线（黑色），一根neutral（白色），一根ground（绿/裸铜）
240V
使用双极断路器（double pole），接两根hot线，通常无neutral（纯240V设备）
240V+N
接两根hot + neutral + ground（干衣机等需要120V+240V的设备）

▶
⚠️ 压力测试超过½ psig时，必须将炉子（和气阀）与管道系统断开！气阀最高承受½ psig测试压力。
高压测试 > ½ psig

"For pressure testing above one-half psig, the furnace and its individual shutoff valve must be disconnected from the gas supply piping system.
