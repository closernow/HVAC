Identify Furnace Components & Explain Their Function
零件识别 + 功能说明 — 指向实物，说出功能
▶
💡
Point clearly at each physical component while speaking. The examiner wants to see you can locate AND explain the function in one confident statement.
HSI — Hot Surface Igniter
热表面点火器
Energized at 115 VAC for a 17-second warm-up period. Heats to ~900°C to ignite gas at the burners. Replaces standing pilot.
Say this
"This is the Hot Surface Igniter. It's energized at 115 volts for approximately 17 seconds to reach ignition temperature — around 900 degrees Celsius. Cold resistance should be between 40 and 70 ohms."
Gas Valve / Valve Train (GV)
气阀 / 阀列
Opens on a 24 VAC signal from the control board via relay GVR. Controls gas flow to the burners. Contains an internal pressure regulator (manifold pressure). Factory set: 3.5" WC for NG.
Say this
"This is the gas valve. It opens when the control board energizes the gas valve relay — GVR — supplying 24 volts to the valve coil. It also contains the internal manifold pressure regulator."
HPS — Housing Pressure Switch
壳体压力开关（主压力开关）
Normally Open (N.O.) contact. Closes when inducer motor creates sufficient negative pressure (draft). Ignored for the first minute after gas valve closes per the Carrier sequence. Nominal break point: 0.63–0.73" WC. (Source: manual Service label Fig. 64)
Say this
"This is the housing pressure switch — HPS. It's a normally-open switch that closes when the inducer creates sufficient negative pressure in the collector box, signalling the control board that it's safe to proceed with ignition."
LPS — Low Gas Pressure Switch
低气压保护开关（丙烷系统）
Used on propane systems. Opens if inlet gas pressure drops below safe minimum, preventing incomplete combustion. Factory connected only when LGPS is used (see wiring diagram Fig. 71 note 10).
Say this
"This is the Low Gas Pressure Switch — LGPS — primarily used on propane systems. It opens if the supply gas pressure falls below the minimum required, shutting down the furnace to prevent incomplete combustion."
Pressure Switch (Collector Box — LPS)
集流箱压力开关
First pressure switch to close as inducer comes up to speed. Begins the 15-second pre-purge. Labeled "COLLECTOR BOX-LPS" on the switch (manual p.62). Nominal break: 0.63" WC min.
Say this
"This pressure switch — labeled COLLECTOR BOX LPS — is the first to close as the inducer motor comes up to speed, initiating the 15-second pre-purge period."
Flame Sensor / FSE
火焰传感器
Uses flame rectification to prove burner ignition. Control board reads 4.0–8.0 µA DC (nominal). If flame not proved within 2 seconds, gas valve closes. Retries up to 3 times → Lockout Code 14. Clean with 0000 steel wool only.
Say this
"This is the flame sensing electrode — FSE. It uses flame rectification to prove ignition. The control board looks for 4 to 8 microamps DC. If flame is not proved within 2 seconds, the gas valve closes and the sequence retries up to 3 times before ignition lockout."
Control Board / PCB
控制板 / 电路板
The "brain" of the furnace. Controls entire sequence of operation, monitors all safety switches, displays 2-digit LED fault codes. Contains 3-amp automotive-type blade fuse (SEC-1 & SEC-2 connections = 24V secondary). Status LED is amber. (Fig. 36, Fig. 64)
Say this
"This is the furnace control board. It manages the entire sequence of operation, monitors all safety switch inputs, and displays 2-digit LED fault codes visible through the blower door window. It contains a 3-amp blade fuse protecting the 24-volt secondary circuit."
Limit Switches — LS1, LS2
过热限位开关
Auto-reset. Opens on over-temperature condition. Blower runs for up to 4 minutes to cool exchanger when limit trips (Code 33). Manual-reset flame rollout switch is also on burner assembly — requires physical button reset. (Service label Fig. 64)
Say this
"These are the limit switches — LS1 and LS2. They are auto-reset and open on over-temperature. When a limit trips, the blower continues to run for up to 4 minutes to cool the heat exchanger. The flame rollout switch on the burner assembly, by contrast, requires manual reset."
Inducer Motor (IDM)
引风机 / 诱导送风机
First component energized in every heating call. Creates negative pressure to close pressure switches. Also provides post-purge for 15 seconds after gas valve closes. 115 VAC motor. (Sequence of Operation, manual p.69)
Say this
"This is the inducer draft motor — IDM. It is the first component energized on every call for heat. It creates negative pressure to close the pressure switches and also runs for a 15-second post-purge after the gas valve closes."
Transformer (TRAN)
变压器
Steps 115 VAC (primary, PR-1) down to 24 VAC (secondary, SEC-1 & SEC-2) for the control circuit. SEC-1 feeds the 3-amp fuse. Visible on control board Fig. 36 as "115-VAC (PR-1) TRANSFORMER PRIMARY".
Say this
"This is the transformer. It steps 115 volts line voltage down to 24 volts for the low-voltage control circuit. The primary is 115 VAC; the secondary feeds the 3-amp fuse and the control board."
Condensate Trap
冷凝水收集器
Unique to condensing furnaces. Collects acidic condensate from secondary heat exchanger. MUST be primed with 1 quart (1 litre) of water before first startup — pour into collector box port (manual p.51, Fig. 59). Drain line: min 1/4" per foot slope. Drain via 1/2" CPVC to code-approved drain.
Say this
"This is the condensate trap — specific to condensing furnaces. It collects acidic condensate from the secondary heat exchanger and must be primed with one quart of water before startup. The condensate drain line requires a minimum slope of one-quarter inch per foot toward the drain."
Blower Motor (BLWM)
循环风机
Starts 25 seconds after flame is proved (blower-ON delay). Distributes conditioned air through duct system. After thermostat is satisfied: runs for blower-OFF delay — factory set 120 seconds (adjustable 90/120/150/180 sec via J1 jumper, Table 20). Multiple speed taps: HEAT, COOL, FAN, SPARE. (manual p.55 & Fig. 36)
Say this
"This is the circulating blower motor. It starts 25 seconds after flame is proved to allow the heat exchanger to warm up. After the thermostat is satisfied, it continues for the blower-off delay — factory set at 120 seconds — to extract residual heat from the exchanger."

▶
💡

Heating Mode — from manual p.69
1
Thermostat calls for heat — R-to-W circuit closes
Say this
"The wall thermostat closes the R-to-W circuit, applying 24 volts to the furnace control board. The board performs a self-check, confirming the collector box pressure switch LPS and housing pressure switch HPS are both open."
2
Inducer Motor starts — Pre-Purge Period (15 sec)
Say this
"The control board energizes the inducer motor IDM. As it comes up to speed, the collector box pressure switch LPS closes, beginning a 15-second pre-purge period to clear the heat exchanger of any residual gases."
Source: manual p.69 — "As the inducer motor IDM comes up to speed, the collector box pressure switch contacts LPS close to begin a 15-sec prepurge period."
3
HPS closes — HSI Warm-Up (17 sec)
Say this
"At the end of the pre-purge period, the inducer housing pressure switch HPS closes, confirming adequate draft. The hot surface igniter HSI is then energized at 115 volts for a 17-second warm-up period."
Source: manual p.69 — "At the end of the prepurge period, the Hot-Surface igniter HSI is energized for a 17-sec igniter warm-up period."
4
Trial-for-Ignition — Gas Valve opens
Say this
"When the igniter warm-up is complete, the main gas valve relay GVR closes, energizing the gas valve GV. Gas flows to the burners and is ignited by the HSI. 24 VAC is also supplied to the humidifier terminal HUM at this point."
Source: manual p.69 — "the main gas valve relay contacts GVR close to energize the gas valve GV."
5
Flame Proving — FSE confirms ignition (2 sec window)
Say this
"Five seconds after GVR closes, the 2-second flame proving period begins. The flame sensor FSE uses flame rectification to confirm the burner flame is present. If flame is not proved, the gas valve closes and the board retries the ignition sequence up to 3 more times before going to Ignition Lockout — Code 14."
6
Blower-ON Delay (25 sec) — BLWM starts on HEAT speed
Say this
"When flame is proved, the control board starts the blower-ON delay — 25 seconds. This allows the heat exchanger to warm up before distributing air. After 25 seconds, the blower motor starts on the HEAT speed tap."
Source: manual p.69 — "the furnace control CPU begins the blower-ON delay period and continues to hold the gas valve GV open."
7
Thermostat satisfied — Blower-OFF Delay (120 sec default)
Say this
"When the thermostat is satisfied, the R-to-W circuit opens, de-energizing gas valve GV and the humidifier terminal. The inducer runs for a 15-second post-purge. The blower motor and EAC-1 terminal remain energized for the blower-OFF delay — factory set at 120 seconds — then shut down."
Source: manual p.69 — "The inducer motor IDM will remain energized for a 15-sec post-purge period. The blower motor BLWM… will remain energized for 90, 120, 150, or 180 sec depending on the blower-OFF delay selection."

Faults the Exam Sheet Names — What to Look For
🔴
Open High Limit
高限位断路
▶
SymptomFurnace does not start, or shuts off mid-cycle. Blower runs up to 4 min. LED Code 33.
MeasureMeasure across limit switch terminals (line voltage present = switch open). Normal = 0V (closed). Fault = 24V (open).
CausesDirty filter, blocked return air, failed blower motor, blower belt slipping, over-fired, restricted ducts.
Say this
"I'm measuring across the high limit switch. I read 24 volts — this tells me the switch contacts are open. The limit has tripped due to an over-temperature condition. I need to find and correct the root cause before resetting."
🔴
HSI Failure
点火器故障
▶
SymptomInducer runs, pressure switch closes, but no ignition. Three retries then Code 14 Ignition Lockout.
Measure (power ON)Check 115V at HSI connector during warm-up window (17 sec). If 115V present but no glow → HSI failed.
Measure (power OFF)Disconnect HSI. Set meter to Ω. Normal = 40–70Ω. OL (open) = failed igniter.
Say this
"With power on, I measured 115 volts at the HSI connector during the warm-up period — the board is commanding the igniter. I'll now de-energize, confirm zero volts, isolate the igniter, and measure resistance. It reads open circuit — the igniter has failed and needs replacement."
📌
Carrier manual (p.65–66): "Check igniter resistance. Nominal resistance is 40 to 70 ohms at room temperature… Cold reading should be between 40 ohms and 70 ohms."
🔴
Open Neutral Wire
中性线断路
▶
SymptomFurnace completely dead — LED status light OFF.
MeasureTest 115V at L1 and L2 at furnace junction box. If L1 hot to ground = 120V but L1 to L2 = 0V → neutral (L2/WHT) open.
Say this
"The LED status light is completely off. I'll first check for 115 volts at the furnace disconnect. I read 115V from L1 to ground, but 0V from L1 to L2 — this indicates the neutral wire or L2 connection is open. I'll trace back to the panel."
🔴
Pressure Switch Failure
压力开关故障 — LED Code 31
▶
SymptomInducer runs but sequence does not advance. HSI never energized. LED Code 31.
MeasureWhile inducer runs, measure across HPS terminals. Normal = 0V (closed). Fault = 24V (switch stuck open).
ChecksInspect pressure tubing for blockage, kinks, or condensate. Check inducer operation. Manual p.62: "Check for: Obstructed pressure tubing — Pressure switch stuck closed."
Say this
"With the inducer running, I'm measuring across the housing pressure switch. I read 24 volts — the switch contacts are still open. The inducer is running but the switch is not closing. I'll check the pressure tubing for blockage or condensate, and verify the inducer is actually creating draft."
🔴
Broken "W" Wire
W线（供热呼叫线）断路
▶
Symptom24V system normal, thermostat set above room temp, but furnace does not respond.
MeasureAt control board W terminal vs COM: should be 24V when T-stat calls. Reading 0V = broken W wire or T-stat contact open.
Say this
"The 24-volt supply is confirmed at the transformer secondary. The thermostat is calling for heat, but I read 0 volts at the W terminal on the control board. The heating call is not reaching the board — I'll check the W wire continuity from the thermostat sub-base to the furnace."
🔴
Ventor (Inducer) Motor Failure
引风机电机故障 — LED Code 31
▶
SymptomNo inducer sound. Pressure switches never close. Code 31.
Measure (power ON)Check 115V at IDM motor terminals. 115V present but motor not running → motor failed. 0V → control board not commanding motor / IDM relay (DR) fault.
Say this
"There is no inducer motor noise. I'll measure voltage at the inducer motor terminals. I read 115 volts — the board is commanding the motor — but the motor is not running. This indicates the inducer motor itself has failed."

Explain "Allowable Vent Length" & "Equivalent Length"
允许排气长度 & 等效长度
▶
Say this — Allowable Vent Length
"Allowable vent length is the maximum length of venting pipe permitted by the manufacturer for a specific appliance model and pipe diameter. For this furnace, it is found in Table 13 — Maximum Equivalent Vent Length. Exceeding this length reduces draft, causes poor combustion, nuisance lockouts, and potential vent freeze-up."
Say this — Equivalent Length
"Equivalent length accounts for the additional flow resistance created by elbows and fittings. A 90-degree long-radius elbow, for example, adds 3 feet of equivalent resistance. A mitered 90-degree elbow adds 8 feet. We add equivalent lengths to actual pipe lengths to get the Total Equivalent Vent Length — TEVL — and compare it to the Maximum Equivalent Vent Length — MEVL."

"If the total equivalent vent length exceeds the manufacturer's maximum, or if we fail to account for the equivalent length of fittings, the system will have inadequate draft — resulting in incomplete combustion, nuisance pressure switch lockouts, condensate freeze-up at the vent termination, and potential carbon monoxide spillage."

🏠
Furnace Operation — Atmospheric Burner Design, Primary & Secondary Air, Venting, Air Supply, Draft Diverter & Smoke Test, Efficiency
自然通风炉工作原理
▶
Say this — Overview
"A natural draft furnace uses an atmospheric burner that draws combustion air by natural convection. Primary air is mixed with gas at the burner orifice through a venturi effect. Secondary air surrounds the flame and completes combustion. Hot combustion products rise by natural buoyancy up through the heat exchanger, through the draft diverter, and up the flue — no fan is required for venting. Efficiency is approximately 60 to 80 percent AFUE because the flue gas leaves at high temperature, carrying significant heat up the chimney."
Draft Diverter — Purpose & Smoke Test
Say this — Draft Diverter
"The draft diverter serves three functions: it relieves sudden down-drafts that could extinguish the pilot flame; it dilutes flue gases with room air to prevent chimney deterioration; and it maintains a constant, stable draft at the heat exchanger regardless of variations in chimney pull."
Say this — Smoke / Spillage Test
"To perform the spillage test, I run the furnace for at least 5 minutes, then hold a smoke match or candle at the relief opening of the draft diverter. If smoke is drawn in — toward the furnace — draft is functioning correctly. If smoke is pushed out — spillage — combustion gases are entering the living space. I must immediately shut down the furnace and investigate the cause."
📋
Sequence of Operation — Natural Draft Furnace
自然通风炉操作顺序
▶
1
Thermostat calls for heat — R-to-W closes — 24V to gas valve
Say this
"The thermostat closes the R-to-W circuit, applying 24 volts to the gas valve coil. On a standing pilot system, the pilot is already burning, so the main gas valve opens immediately."
2
Gas valve opens — main burners ignite from pilot
Say this
"The gas valve opens, gas flows to the main burners, and the standing pilot ignites the main burner flame. There is no inducer motor — combustion gases rise by natural buoyancy through the heat exchanger and up the flue."
3
Heat exchanger warms → Fan switch closes → Blower starts
Say this
"As the heat exchanger warms, the fan switch bimetal element within the fan/limit control reaches its set point — typically around 130°F — and closes, starting the circulating blower motor to distribute warm air. The fan switch is temperature-actuated, not time-delayed."
4
Thermostat satisfied → Gas valve closes → Blower continues until cool
Say this
"When the thermostat is satisfied, the gas valve closes and the burners shut off. The blower continues to run until the heat exchanger cools enough for the fan switch to open — typically around 90 to 100°F — then the blower shuts down."

Identify Components: Gas Valve Coil, Transformer, Thermostat Circuits & Contacts — Indicate Expected Voltages
零件识别 + 预期电压

Components — Natural Draft Furnace
Limit / Fan Switch
风机/限位开关
Combined bimetal device. Fan section: temperature-actuated (closes ~130°F, opens ~90°F). Limit section: over-temp protection, opens at ~200°F for forced warm-air furnace, cutting 24V to gas valve.
Blower Motor
循环风机
Started by fan switch closing (temp-actuated). Runs until exchanger cools. Multiple speed taps selectable.
Pilot / Thermocouple
长明火 / 热电偶
Standing pilot burns continuously. Thermocouple tip in flame generates 25–30 mV to hold gas valve safety seat open.
Heat Exchanger
热交换器
Single-stage (primary only). Hot combustion gases flow through; return air flows over the outside surface, picking up heat.
Draft Diverter
调风板
Relieves down-draft, dilutes flue gas, maintains stable heat exchanger draft. Located between heat exchanger outlet and flue connector.
Gas Valve
气阀
24V coil. Includes thermocouple safety seat (millivolt), main gas operator, and pilot operator. Verify pilot valve is correct for NG or LP.

▶
Say this — Function
"The heat anticipator is a small fixed or adjustable resistor inside a mechanical thermostat bimetal. How to Set the Anticipator — Ammeter Method
1
Remove thermostat from sub-base (do not disconnect wires — keep circuit active).
2

3
Ensure thermostat is calling for heat (thermostat leads jumpered at sub-base R-W if needed).
4
Record ammeter reading. Divide by 10 (because of 10 turns) to get true current.
5
Set anticipator dial to that value.
True Current (A) = Ammeter Reading ÷ 10 turns
Example: Ammeter reads 5.0A → 5.0 ÷ 10 = 0.5A → set anticipator to 0.5

Provides the reference set-point force. Clockwise = compress spring = increase outlet pressure. Counter-clockwise = reduce pressure.

Measuring Element (Diaphragm)

Flexible membrane that senses outlet pressure. Moves upward when outlet pressure increases (restricting orifice), downward when outlet pressure drops (opening orifice).

大气排气口
Allows the space below the diaphragm to communicate with atmosphere, enabling the diaphragm to move freely in response to pressure changes. If blocked — by ice, insects, or debris — the regulator cannot regulate correctly.

"The atmospheric vent allows the underside of the diaphragm to reference atmospheric pressure, so it can accurately sense and respond to outlet pressure variations."

Gas Rate (m³/h) = 3600 ÷ Seconds per revolution × Dial size (m³)

BTU/h = Gas Rate (m³/h) × 35,310
Alternatively: Gas Rate (ft³/h) = 3600 ÷ seconds × dial size (ft³)  |  BTU/h = ft³/h × Heating Value (approx. 1,000 BTU/ft³ for NG)
1

2
Run furnace in heating mode.
3
Use the 2-cubic-foot test dial (or smallest available metric dial).
4
Time 3 complete needle revolutions — record each time in seconds. Take average.
5
Apply formula: Rate = 3600 ÷ avg_seconds × dial_size
6
Multiply rate by heating value to get BTU/h. Compare to furnace rating plate.
7
Acceptable: within ±2% of nameplate BTU/h rating.

Say this — Grounding and Bonding
"Grounding connects the electrical system to earth ground at the main panel — providing a path for fault current and protection against lightning. Bonding connects all metallic components — gas piping, electrical panel, ductwork, water piping — together so they are at the same electrical potential. This prevents dangerous voltage differences that could cause shock or sparks near gas piping. Per CSA C22.1, the furnace cabinet must have an uninterrupted equipment ground. (Carrier manual p.25: 'The cabinet MUST have an uninterrupted or unbroken ground.')"
Breaker Installation Steps
1
Turn off main breaker before working in panel.
2
Select correct breaker size for wire gauge and circuit load.
3
Strip wire insulation to correct length — no scoring of conductors.
4
Connect: hot (black) → breaker terminal; neutral (white) → neutral bus bar; ground (green/bare) → ground bus bar.
5
Snap breaker into panel bus. Verify firm seating.
6
For receptacle: connect black (hot) → brass screw; white (neutral) → silver screw; bare/green → green ground screw.
7
Tug test all connections. No bare conductor exposed beyond terminals.
⚠️
Carrier manual p.25: "Do not connect aluminum wire between disconnect switch and furnace. Use only copper wire." CSA C22.1 prohibits aluminum wire on 15A and 20A branch circuits.
Leak Testing & Purging — Code Reference
Say this — pressure testing above ½ PSIG
"For pressure testing above one-half PSIG — 14 inches WC — the furnace and its individual manual shutoff valve must be completely disconnected from the gas supply piping. This is because the gas valve is not rated for high-pressure testing and could be damaged or allow gas to pass. The pipe ends are capped, pressure is applied, and the system is checked with soap solution for leaks."
🔍
Leak Testing & Purging Procedures
漏气测试 + 清管程序
▶
High pressure (> ½ PSIG)
Disconnect furnace from gas supply piping. Cap pipe ends. Pressurize system. Check all joints with soap solution. If pointer on meter dial moves after tape-marking → leak present.
Low pressure (≤ ½ PSIG)
Close manual shutoff valve at furnace (no need to disconnect). Apply test pressure. Check all joints with soap solution and/or dial test.
Purging
After pressure test passes: purge air from new piping using approved purging tool — no ignition sources present. Confirm gas presence by detector or smell. Perform final soap test at all joints with system at operating pressure.
